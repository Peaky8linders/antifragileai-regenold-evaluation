## Reading

- **Every headline axis is up vs the R369 baseline.** ref_corr +10pp
  (7W/1L — the collapse/promote + R329 restore targets), ans_corr +6.7pp,
  ans_faith +16.7pp, ans_conc +3.4pp. The merged main strictly dominates the
  R369 baseline on 5 of 6 measured axes.
- **Post-merge vs pre-merge ref_corr:** 0.433 → 0.450, net +1 flip
  (xr_06/med_03/med_5/ng_05 win, xr_12/med_10/med_8 lose) — Stage-2
  generation is stochastic; the merge (R328/R342/R344 restorations + R371)
  did not regress the wire.
- **The two ans_rel losses are noise, not damage:**
  - `graphrag:ng_02` — PRE and POST answers are **byte-identical**; the judge
    flipped pass→fail on relevance. Pure judge self-inconsistency (measured
    earlier at ~1.2%).
  - `graphrag:med_2` — a pre-existing R329 citable-block refusal (the row's
    citable list omits Art. 17; the model refuses to elaborate on QMS content
    it cannot cite). Same refusal pre-merge; the terse form loses cite/faith/
    rel verdicts. This is a retrieval-side citable-list gap for that one row,
    not a regression from the merge — tracked as a candidate for a future
    supplement, do not revert the R329 block over it.

## Judge-comparability caveat

`r370-bedrock-golden-live.json` (written by the runner's inline
`evals.judge.grounded` call) uses a DIFFERENT verdict shape
(correct/incorrect/unsupported) than the R369 baseline's 7-axis script
(supported/contradicted/factual_score) — do not quote it against any baseline.
The apples-to-apples numbers are the three `*-7axis.json` files above.
