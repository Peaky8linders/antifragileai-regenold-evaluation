# R371.8 — citation-minimality post-processor: measured, VETOED (blind form)

**Date:** 2026-08-18 · **Ask:** the R371.7 §4 recommendation #2 / R369 pointer —
measure how many gold-dropping rows improve if the citation post-processor
caps refs to fired anchors. · **Pool:** the 140-row R371.5 gate artifact
(`dynamic-ab-r371-roleobl-FINAL.json`, baseline arm), scored with the SAME
deterministic functions the harness uses (`evals/bench/metrics.py`), plus the
qwen3-32b judge verdicts from `r371-roleobl-judged.json`.

**Verdict: the blind prose-cap is VETOED (hard rule #8) — net-negative on every
axis. The granularity-collapse lever is invisible to the deterministic scorer
(judge-visible only). No post-processor ships from this measurement.**

---

## 1. Levers measured

- **A — granularity collapse** (R369 "dedupe to most-specific form"; the
  R371.4 wire-collapse instruction): leaf+head pairs (`Article 50.1` +
  `Article 50`) → head. Head-set-preserving by construction.
- **B — prose-cap** ("cap to fired anchors", operationalised as the R368
  wire-guard's support test in the minimality direction): drop refs whose
  head the answer prose never names.

## 2. Results (140 rows, baseline arm)

| metric (mean) | baseline | A collapse | B prose-cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| ref_loose | 0.8670 | 0.8670 | **0.8108 (−0.056)** |
| ref_strict | 0.7280 | 0.7280 | **0.6932 (−0.035)** |
| ref_conc | 0.6000 | 0.6000 | **0.5857 (−0.014)** |
| gold_dropped_head SUM | 30 | 30 | **43 (+13)** |

- **Gold-dropping rows at baseline: 27 of 140.**
- **Lever A: exactly zero movement on every axis.** The deterministic scorer
  counts HEADS, so collapsing leaf+head changes nothing it can see. The judge
  reads the raw list (R369: "50.1 + 50.3 reads as two refs"), so A is
  judge-visible only — an offline deterministic measurement cannot see it.
  (The wire-level collapse already shipped; a judge-based A/B is the only way
  to measure it.)
- **Lever B: 10 rows improve, 12 rows lose gold heads (+13).** The 12 veto
  rows are the "cite without discuss" family — gold heads cited in refs but
  never named in the answer prose (the cite_faith MISMATCHED class).

## 3. Judge view — why the veto is decisive

Of the 12 veto rows, **5 currently PASS judge ref_corr and would flip to
FAIL** under the blind cap: `graphrag:gt_08`, `graphrag:ng_07`,
`live_answers:la_q13`, `live_answers:la_q6`, `tricky_v2:tr_v2_003`. The other
7 already fail (missing_governing / overinclusion) and lose more gold.

Of the 10 improved rows, 4 pass / 6 fail at baseline; the 6 failing ones fail
for missing_governing — the cap cannot fix recall, so the improvement is
continuous (conc/F1), not verdict-flipping — except `medtech:grb_01`
(judge: "overinclusion" — the cap removes exactly the flagged ref).

## 4. Decision

1. **Blind prose-cap: REJECTED.** +13 gold heads dropped (12 rows, 5 of them
   judge-passing); aggregate ref_loose −0.056, ref_strict −0.035, ref_conc
   −0.014. A gold-aware cap ("drop only refs absent from prose AND gold")
   would improve 10 rows with 0 gold movement — but it needs gold at
   inference time, i.e. an oracle. Not shippable.
2. **Granularity collapse: no offline evidence either way** (deterministic
   scorer is blind to it); the wire-level collapse is already shipped and a
   judge-based A/B is the honest next measurement — not this offline lever.
3. **The real surface is generation-side.** The 12 veto rows need the answer
   to DISCUSS its cited gold heads (Stage-2 prose coverage), not a
   post-processor to drop them. The 10 improved rows' overinclusion failures
   (grb_01) are the precision side, and the correct lever there is the
   rerank + adaptive clamp (R281) — already live — not a prose cap.
