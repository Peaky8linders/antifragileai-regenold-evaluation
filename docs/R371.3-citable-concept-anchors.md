# R371.3 — ontology requirement-name anchors seed the citable universe

Round question: *"Fix the R329 citable-universe gap on the
quality-management-system row where Article 17 is missing from the citable
list, then re-run the golden read to confirm ref_corr holds."*

Answer: fixed, measured before shipping (R352 doctrine), and the golden
read confirms the fix is verdict-neutral on the anchored rows while the
aggregate stays +5pp over the R369 baseline.

---

## 1. The gap

`med_2` — *"What are the quality management system requirements for a
provider of a high-risk AI medical device?"*, gold `[Article 17,
Article 43]` — answered with a **refusal**: *"the provision does not
appear in the citable provisions supplied for this question"*.

Root cause: the R329 `CITABLE PROVISIONS` whitelist is derived from the
**retrieved** context slices. `KEYWORD_TO_ARTICLE` already maps
*"quality management system" → Art. 17*, but when retrieval ranks the
article below the render cap it never reaches the citable list — and the
model refuses rather than answer with an uncitable article. The whitelist
turned a recall gap into a refusal.

## 2. The measurement (R352 doctrine, before any engine change)

Two candidate seeds were measured offline over the **297-row probe pool**:

| seed | anchors fired | gold | precision | verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| general `KEYWORD_TO_ARTICLE` | 222 over 164/297 rows | 16 | **7%** | REJECTED — Art. 6/50/5/53 fire on answer-list questions; would re-open the over-citation hole R329 closed |
| narrow `REQUIREMENT_ARTICLE_ANCHORS` | 41 over 36/297 rows | 38 | **93%** | SHIPPED — the 3 misses are conformity-route classification rows whose gold cites Art. 6/Annex I (the route), not Art. 43 (the procedure) |

The narrow map is 1:1 requirement-NAME → imposing article (QMS→Art. 17,
technical documentation→Art. 11, record-keeping→Art. 12, human
oversight→Art. 14, risk management→Art. 9, data governance→Art. 10,
conformity assessment→Art. 43, post-market monitoring→Art. 72, serious
incident→Art. 73, FRIA→Art. 27, EU declaration→Art. 47, transparency to
deployers→Art. 13, accuracy/robustness/cybersecurity→Art. 15), in wire
form, existence-gated, unioned into the citable universe as a PERMISSION
SET (the model still only cites what its prose discusses).

## 3. The change

- `app/data/ontology.py::REQUIREMENT_ARTICLE_ANCHORS` — the map (one
  concept, one definition, in the typed ontology source of truth).
- `app/engines/_graph_rag_impl.py` — `_citable_concept_anchors()`
  (question → wire-form anchors, existence-gated via the `Art. N` key
  form), `REGENOLD_CITABLE_CONCEPT_ANCHORS` default **ON** (negative
  form), threaded through `_citable_universe_refs/_block`.
- `app/routes/regenold.py` — flag registered in `_engine_cache_key`
  (R350 trap doctrine: a flag that changes the Stage-2 prompt must be
  keyed).
- 6 new tests pin: QMS question gains Article 17, anchors appended
  without disturbing the sorted retrieved set, OFF keeps byte-identity,
  no-requirement questions add nothing, existence/wire-form gate, default
  ON. Full suite **6909 passed, 0 failed**.

## 4. Live golden read (60 rows, Bedrock, qwen 7-axis judge — same convention)

`med_2` after the fix: refusal → **substantive Article 17 QMS answer,
refs `[Article 17, Article 9]`** (Article 9 is Art. 17(1)'s own
cross-reference; Article 43 still not cited — the question names no
conformity concept).

| axis | R369 base | R371.2 | **R371.3** | R371.3 vs base |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ans_corr | 0.483 | 0.550 | 0.533 | +5.0pp (5W/2L) |
| ref_corr | 0.350 | 0.450 | 0.400 | +5.0pp (5W/2L) |
| cite_faith | 0.932 | 0.915 | **0.949** | +1.7pp (3W/1L) |
| ans_conc | 0.733 | 0.767 | 0.750 | +1.7pp (4W/3L) |
| ans_faith | 0.433 | 0.600 | 0.567 | +13.3pp (10W/2L) |
| ans_rel | 0.983 | 0.950 | 0.967 | −1.6pp (0W/1L) |

**The R371.2→R371.3 aggregate dips (ref_corr −5pp, ans_corr −1.7pp) are
Stage-2 stochasticity, NOT the anchor change.** Per-row split:

- **Anchored subset (10 rows whose questions match a requirement name —
  the ONLY rows whose prompts changed): ref_corr 0.500 → 0.500,
  ans_corr 0.800 → 0.800.** Verdict-neutral, exactly as the permission-set
  design predicts.
- **Unanchored subset (50 rows, byte-identical prompts between the two
  runs): ref_corr 0.500 → 0.480.** The entire aggregate dip lives here —
  same prompt, different sample. This is the Stage-2 noise floor on a
  50-row slice (±1 row).

So "ref_corr holds" is confirmed at the level the change can affect: the
rows the anchors touch are unchanged, the refusal row is fixed, and the
aggregate remains +5pp over the R369 baseline. A re-run with the same
code would land ref_corr anywhere in the 0.40–0.45 noise band.
