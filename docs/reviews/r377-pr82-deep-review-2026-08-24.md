## Critical / Important

### [C1] The denoiser's truncation guard is dead on the native Bedrock path
- **File:** `app/routes/regenold.py:7251`
- **Bug:** `if getattr(resp, "finish_reason", None) == "length":` — but
  `bedrock_client.py:596` sets `finish_reason=stop_reason or None` from the raw Converse
  `stopReason`, whose truncation value is `"max_tokens"`.
- **Reproduced by execution, three independent routes:** feeding a Converse reply with
  `stopReason='max_tokens'` through the real `_parse_converse_response` → guard False, the
  53-char fragment passes the `10 <= len <= 500` bound; driving the real
  `_rewrite_multiturn_query` → `fired=True`, truncated fragment returned as the retrieval
  query; a one-token A/B → `max_tokens` returns the fragment, `length` returns `None`.
- **The character bound cannot backstop it:** 100 tokens ≈ 400–480 chars, so a `max_tokens`
  cut can essentially never exceed the 500-char ceiling.
- **NARROWING (do not overstate):** dead on the *Converse* path only.
  `bedrock_client.py:1073` copies `finish_reason` from an `OpenAIWrapperResponse` on the
  Claude-Max hop, so the value there *is* `"length"` and the guard *does* fire.
  `BedrockResponse.finish_reason` carries two vocabularies in one field.
- **The remedy is NOT "restore a proven protection".** R91 is NO-RECORD (below). The valid
  argument is narrower and survives anyway: **R91 itself handled both vocabularies** —
  commit `8a7ef61` lists `_anthropic_complete_for_graph_rag returns None on max_tokens
  stop` — and the denoiser got only the `length` half because in 2026-05 its chain had no
  Anthropic-family link. R377 added one without revisiting the comparison.
- **Repo consistency:** every other truncation guard is either vocabulary-matched
  (`_graph_rag_impl.py:1002` `== "max_tokens"`) or backed by a structural detector
  (`fusion.py:730` pairs `== "length"` with `_looks_truncated`). The denoiser's Bedrock link
  is the sole guard with **neither**.
- **Fix:** normalise the truncation signal **once**, in `_BedrockDenoiserProvider` or
  `bedrock_client` — not by adding a second literal at the comparison site — and add the
  structural backstop its siblings have. **Gate it, default OFF**, until the truncation rate
  is measured (see Owed #1): with fall-through enabled, a caught truncation now routes to
  salvage → concatenation, and the audits *disagree on whether that is better*. One measured
  case had the dead guard produce the right entities (`Art. 26`) and the fixed guard the
  wrong ones (`Annex III`). Both directions are unmeasured and both land on scored axes.
