# R371.7 — Article 13 recall supplement: measured, REJECTED

**Date:** 2026-08-18 · **Target:** the `la_q45` gap — deployer-obligation answers that
discuss "instructions for use" cite Article 26(1) but not Article 13, while gold
`['Annex III', 'Article 13', 'Article 26', 'Article 27']` demands it. · **Ask:** a
measured recall supplement, trigger precision measured offline BEFORE shipping.

**Verdict: no shippable trigger exists. The supplement is REJECTED on measurement —
no flag ships.** The failure is not the trigger engineering; it is that the gold
itself disagrees on this family, so any deterministic trigger that recovers
`la_q45` necessarily mis-fires on its gold-inconsistent twins.

---

## 1. Mechanism candidates and their measured precision

All measurements are offline, head-level, over the 297-row probe pool and the
140-row R371.5 gate artifact (real generated answers + gold).

### Q1 — question-side regex trigger (the R368 pattern)

| trigger | fires | recover (gold has Art 13) | FP | precision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `deployer` + `obligation/owe/duty` | 8 | 2 | 6 | **25%** |
| + `high-risk` | 5 | 2 | 3 | **40%** |
| `obligations` + `deployer` | 8 | 2 | 6 | **25%** |

The FPs are the same family as the recoveries: `ng_03` ("What obligations exist
for deployers of high-risk AI systems?", gold `[26, 27]`), `grb_07` (hospital
deployer, gold `[26, 27]`), `la_q39` (transparency to deployers, gold `[50]`).

### Q2 — gold-answer prose trigger ("mentions instructions for use")

Over the 137 rows with gold answers: **7 fires, 5 recover, 2 FP = 71%**. The FPs
are structural: `ma_07` (a *comparison of named articles* question, gold exactly
`[16, 26]`) and `grb_07` (the gold-inconsistent hospital twin).

### Q3 — generated-answer prose trigger (the real wire mechanism)

Over the 140-row gate artifact (baseline arm), answers whose prose mentions
"instructions for use": **18 fires → 1 RECOVER (`la_q45`), 4 already cite Art 13
correctly, 8 mention it without citing (correct), 5 ALREADY over-cite Art 13
against gold that excludes it** (`st_v4_011` gold `[27]`, `mt_v4_010` gold `[86]`,
`med_10` gold `[99]`, `grb_10` gold `[14]`, `mt_v4_008` gold `[10]`).

The supplement's actual effect would be to move the 8 NOOP rows toward the
over-citation class — the system already over-cites Article 13 on instruction-
discussing prose (5/18 rows), so making it citable on a broader trigger amplifies
that direction, not the recall direction. Net measured recovery: 1 row.

---

## 2. Root cause: gold variance, not a retrieval gap

The deployer-obligation family's gold is **internally inconsistent across
datasets**:

- `ng_03` (graphrag) ≈ `la_q45` (live_answers) — near-identical questions,
  gold `[26, 27]` vs `[Annex III, 13, 26, 27]`.
- `grb_07` (medtech) ≈ `la_q71` (live_answers) — hospital-deployer twins, gold
  `[26, 27]` vs `[Annex III, 13, 25, 26, 27, 86]`.

Verified systematically: **4 exact-string duplicate questions across
medtech/live_answers carry divergent golds**:

| question | medtech gold | live_answers gold |
|---|---|---|
| ambient scribe risk tier | `[50]` | `[Annex III, 6]` |
| wellness chatbot risk tier | `[50]` | `[Annex I, 50, 6]` |
| oncology-treatment provider lifecycle | `[Annex III, 43, 6, 9]` | `[Annex I, III, 10, 13, 15, 16, 17, 19, 25, 49, 6, 9]` |
| clinical-trial patient selection | `[Annex III, 6]` | `[Annex I, III, 5, 50, 6]` |

The live_answers gold (expert-corrected) is consistently a rich superset of the
medtech gold (paper ground truth, leaner). No question-side or answer-side
feature separates `la_q45` from `ng_03` — they are paraphrases — so the
R352/KNOWN_DIVERGENCES doctrine applies in its sharpest form: **"legally correct
is not gold-correct", and here even the gold is not self-consistent.**

---

## 3. Decision

- **No flag ships.** All three trigger families fall below the R368 bar (100%
  trigger precision, 0 FPs) and the Q3 projection shows the lever would push
  NOOP rows into the pre-existing over-citation class.
- `la_q45` stays as-is. Its gold is the richer live_answers variant; the system
  cannot know which gold a generic deployer-obligation question carries.
- This closes R371.6 follow-up #1 ("la_q45-class Art. 13 recall") as
  **measured-rejected**.

1. **Reconcile the duplicate-gold pairs** (§2 table + `ng_03`/`la_q45`,
   `grb_07`/`la_q71`): the medtech rows are leaner than the expert-corrected
   live_answers twins. Either adopt the rich gold for both or record the
   variance deliberately — until then, no recall supplement on this family can
   be measured honestly.
2. ~~**The over-citation direction is the real surface**~~ — **MEASURED
   (R371.8): the blind prose-cap post-processor is REJECTED.** Over the
   140-row gate artifact it drops +13 gold heads across 12 rows (5 of them
   judge-PASSING) for a net ref_strict −0.035; the granularity-collapse lever
   is invisible to the deterministic scorer (judge-visible only). The 5 rows
   that over-cite Article 13 here are the precision side, whose correct lever
   is the live rerank + R281 adaptive clamp — not a prose cap. Record:
   `docs/R371.8-minimality-measured.md`.
